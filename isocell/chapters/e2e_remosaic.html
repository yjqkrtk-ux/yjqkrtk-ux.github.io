How Heterogeneous Parallelization Works on the Camera ISP Pipeline

In a mobile camera system, the sensor transmits the captured 200MP non-Bayer RAW frame into SoC memory over MIPI. Once the RAW frame is in memory, the system must perform both DL Remosaic (converting the $4\times 4$ non-Bayer mosaic into Bayer) and Camera ISP processing (Demosaic, Denoising, Sharpening, Tone Mapping, and Color Conversion to YUV—collectively cfa2yuv).

  • Conventional Serial Pipeline (~3.0s Latency): In a conventional setup, the system runs full-frame DL Remosaic first, writes the entire 200MP Bayer frame back to DRAM, and only then starts the sequential Camera ISP pipeline. Because 200 million pixels must pass through Remosaic and ISP stages one after another, end-to-end cfa2yuv latency reaches ~3 seconds.
  • E2E AI Remosaic — Mounting Distributed CPU/GPU/NPU/ISP Execution on the ISP Pipeline (~1.0s Latency, up to 3× Faster): Instead of running Remosaic as an isolated pre-processing step before the ISP, E2E AI Remosaic jointly optimizes DL Remosaic and S/W ISP, distributes their sub-tasks across the mobile SoC's heterogeneous compute engines (CPU, NPU, GPU, and H/W ISP), and mounts them directly onto the Camera ISP pipeline. While the NPU executes deep learning Remosaic inference on active frame regions, the GPU, CPU, and ISP concurrently execute downstream Camera ISP stages (cfa2yuv) in a pipelined manner across Qualcomm Snapdragon and MediaTek Dimensity platforms.
