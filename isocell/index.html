CHAPTER 01 · PILLAR 1 (APR 2021 – PRESENT)

200MP ISOCELL Sensor Development — HP2 (0.6µm) · HP3/HP9 (0.56µm) · HP5 (0.5µm)

Core Principle: Converting non-Bayer multi-cell Color Filter Arrays (4×4 Hexadeca / 2×2 Q-cell) into a standard Bayer pattern for full 200MP capture and 2×/4× In-Sensor Zoom.
Development History: Chronological track record from ISOCELL HP2 (in-sensor AI 4×4 Q-cell Remosaic QMSC for Galaxy S23 Ultra 4× zoom) and ISOCELL HP3/HP9 (world's 1st DL Hexadeca 16-cell Remosaic S/W for main & telephoto cameras) to ISOCELL HP5 (Technical Leader for the world's smallest 0.5µm 200MP sensor).

Remosaic Principle HP2 · QMSC (Apr 2021) HP3/HP9 · 1st DL 16-Cell (Sep 2022) HP5 · 0.5µm Tech Lead (Jan 2025)
CHAPTER 02 · PILLAR 2 (SEP 2022 – PRESENT)

E2E AI Remosaic (Gen 1–4) — Heterogeneous SoC Parallelization on the ISP Pipeline

Core Principle: Distributing heavy DL Remosaic and Camera ISP workloads across mobile CPU / GPU / NPU / ISP and mounting them directly onto the Camera ISP pipeline to execute in parallel—accelerating 200MP cfa2yuv capture latency up to 3× faster (3s → 1s @ SM8550).
Feature Evolution: Chronological progression from Gen 1 (foundational turn-key ISP) and Gen 2 (Heterogeneous SoC parallelization, SEC 2023 Silver Award) to Gen 3 (In-Sensor Zoom & Multi-frame) and Gen 4 (DL Multi-frame Remosaic on flagship AP NPU).
