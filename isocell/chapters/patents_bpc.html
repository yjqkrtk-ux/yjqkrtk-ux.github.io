CHAPTER 04 · GRANTED US PATENTS (US 12,574,659 B2 & US 12,363,458 B2)

Neural Bad-Pixel & Cluster Defect Restoration in 200MP Sensors

In 200MP sub-micron sensors (0.50µm–0.60µm), single defective pixels and multi-pixel cluster defects inevitably arise from silicon crystal imperfections, dark-current spikes, or PDAF/shielded pixel structures. Classical pre-interpolation blurs local gradients and corrupts the receptive field of downstream neural remosaic networks. This chapter examines two granted US patents invented at Samsung Electronics that solve this problem inside the neural network.

  • Understand US Patent 12,574,659 B2 (Granted Mar 2026): injecting encoded bad-pixel coordinate metadata directly into an intermediate feature layer of a neural ISP network.
  • Understand US Patent 12,363,458 B2 (Granted Jul 2025): deep learning-based cluster bad-pixel correction across non-Bayer 4×4 same-color neighborhoods.
  • Experiment with single and cluster defect patterns in the interactive Neural Bad-Pixel Positional Injection Simulator.

US 12,574,659 B2 — Intermediate-Layer Bad-Pixel Positional Injection

Patent Details: “Image signal processor, image sensor, and operating method of the image sensor” (Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. · Filed Nov 8, 2023 · Granted Mar 10, 2026 · {_ext_link("https://patents.google.com/patent/US12574659B2/en", "Google Patents US12574659B2")}).

If a defective pixel map $M_{\text{BPC}} \in \{0,1\}^{H\times W}$ is simply concatenated at the input layer of a deep CNN, every early convolutional layer must allocate channel capacity to propagate binary mask coordinates, increasing MACs and memory bandwidth. Conversely, if bad pixels are naively replaced before the network, the network cannot distinguish true high-frequency highlights from interpolated defects.

$$\mathbf{F}_{\ell+1} = \sigma\!\left(\mathbf{W}_{\ell} * \mathbf{F}_{\ell} \;+\; \mathcal{E}_{\text{pos}}\!\left(M_{\text{BPC}}\right)\right), \qquad \mathcal{E}_{\text{pos}}(M_{\text{BPC}}) \in \mathbb{R}^{C_\ell \times \frac{H}{s} \times \frac{W}{s}}$$

In US 12,574,659 B2, the sensor or ISP encodes the bad-pixel position data $\mathcal{E}_{\text{pos}}(M_{\text{BPC}})$ and injects it directly into an intermediate layer $\ell$ of the neural network, allowing early feature extractors to remain lightweight while guiding mid-level reconstruction kernels to dynamically re-weight valid neighboring phases.

US 12,363,458 B2 — Deep Learning Cluster Bad-Pixel Correction

Patent Details: “Image sensor and operating method” (Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. · Filed Oct 30, 2023 · Granted Jul 15, 2025 · {_ext_link("https://patents.google.com/patent/US12363458B2/en", "Google Patents US12363458B2")}).

In $4\times 4$ Tetra²pixel / Hexadeca sensors, a cluster defect (e.g., $2\times 2$ or $3\times 3$ adjacent defective photodiodes) can wipe out a large fraction of a single color cluster, causing directional rules to fail along diagonal edges. US 12,363,458 B2 introduces a learned cross-channel context restoration mechanism that leverages intact cross-color structural gradients to reconstruct cluster-corrupted sub-micron neighborhoods without edge haloing.

Key Takeaways · Chapter 04

  1. US 12,574,659 B2 (Granted Mar 2026): Injecting encoded bad-pixel position metadata into an intermediate neural network layer avoids input-channel bloat while preventing defective pixels from contaminating learned remosaic filters.
