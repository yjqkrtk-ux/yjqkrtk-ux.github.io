Sub-Micron 200MP Sensor Physics & Tetra²pixel (4×4) CFA Architecture

Packing 200 million active pixels into a mobile smartphone camera module requires shrinking individual pixel pitch from 0.8µm down to 0.60µm (ISOCELL HP2), 0.56µm (ISOCELL HP3/HP9), and 0.50µm (ISOCELL HP5 — the world's smallest 200MP sensor). At 0.50µm, the pixel width is comparable to the wavelength of green light ($\lambda \approx 0.55\text{ }\mu\text{m}$), making physical silicon-algorithm co-design mandatory.

Optical Diffraction, Full-Well Capacity & Shot-Noise Scaling

When pixel pitch $p$ scales down from $0.60\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ to $0.50\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ inside a $1/1.56\text{''}$ optical format, two physical bottlenecks dominate raw signal fidelity:

$$d_{\text{Airy}} = 2.44\,\lambda\,N, \qquad \text{SNR}_{\text{shot}}(E) = \frac{\eta\,A_{\text{pix}}\,E\,t}{\sqrt{\eta\,A_{\text{pix}}\,E\,t + \sigma_{\text{read}}^2}}, \qquad \text{SNR}_{\max} = 10\log_{10}\!\left(N_{\text{FWC}}\right)$$

At green wavelength $\lambda = 0.55\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ and lens aperture $N = f/1.7$, the diffraction-limited Airy disk diameter is $d_{\text{Airy}} \approx 2.28\text{ }\mu\text{m}$—spanning more than four $0.50\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ pixels ($4 \times 0.50\text{ }\mu\text{m} = 2.00\text{ }\mu\text{m}$). Simultaneously, photon collection area $A_{\text{pix}} = p^2$ drops by $30.6\%$ from $0.60\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ ($0.36\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$) to $0.50\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ ($0.25\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$).

Key Takeaways · Chapter 01

  1. Sub-Micron Physical Ceiling: At $0.50\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (HP5) and $0.56\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (HP3/HP9), the optical Airy disk ($d_{\text{Airy}} \approx 2.3\text{ }\mu\text{m}$) spans an entire $4\times 4$ same-color cluster, meaning classical linear interpolation cannot recover high-frequency chromatic edges without severe aliasing.
  2. Silicon–Algorithm Co-Design: Tetra²pixel hardware binning handles extreme dynamic range and low-light SNR in silicon, while Deep Learning Remosaic (Chapters 02 & 03) reconstructs full 200MP spatial fidelity and lossless 2×/4× in-sensor zoom.
