Ledger Analytics Engine (1_Household_Finance) & Zero-Dependency OOXML Builder

Naively summing authorization logs, billing statements, and bank transfer ledgers exported from heterogeneous financial institutions introduces four major accounting distortions: (1) authorization-vs-billing duplicate entries, (2) expense inflation caused by taking absolute values (abs) of cancellations and refunds, (3) double-counting of internal transfers between own/family accounts, and (4) mixing rigid fixed obligations (financial_burden) with variable operating expenses (pure consumption). 1_Household_Finance resolves all four via the three-stage pipeline parser.py:664 → analyzer.py:14 → xlsx_writer.py:10.

Core Accounting Invariants in analyzer.py

  1. Refund Sign-Preservation Rule (analyzer.py:14): In normalized ledger records, standard outflows carry negative amounts ($\text{amount} \lt 0$) while cancellations and refunds carry positive amounts ($\text{amount} \gt 0$). Applying abs(tx['amount']) during expense summation would miscalculate a $100$-unit payment followed by a $100$-unit refund as $200$ of net spending instead of $0$. Therefore, spend(tx) strictly applies the unary negation operator -tx['amount']: $$\text{spend}(\text{tx}) = -\text{tx}[\text{'amount'}], \qquad \sum_{i} \text{spend}(\text{tx}_i) = \sum_{\text{pay}} |\text{amount}| - \sum_{\text{refund}} |\text{amount}|$$
  2. Dual-Track Aggregation: Pure Expense vs. Fixed Financial Burden (analyzer.py:60, analyzer.py:77): Rigid obligations such as fixed financing costs and taxes (tx['financial_burden'] == True) are separated from variable operating expenses (Pure Expense), producing independent worksheet tabs for real monthly consumption versus total cash outflow.
  3. Internal Transfer Netting (analyzer.py:22) & Complete-Month Coverage Filtering (analyzer.py:120): Transfers between accounts belonging to the same household entity (is_internal) are reconciled and excluded, while month_coverage() filters out partially collected months so they cannot skew annual monthly average benchmarks.
  4. Zero-Dependency Direct OOXML .xlsx Generator (xlsx_writer.py:10): Packages [Content_Types].xml, xl/styles.xml, and xl/worksheets/sheet*.xml directly via Python's built-in zipfile module, running out-of-the-box in any standard Python environment without third-party libraries.

AI Vision Studio (4_Photo_Memory_Studio) Multimodal Architecture

To browse, compare, and curate a massive local media archive safely without re-uploading to external clouds, 4_Photo_Memory_Studio is architected around four core modules:

  • Read-Only Indexer & EXIF Normalization (indexer.py:24): Scans PHOTOS_ROOT in strict Read-Only mode, indexing EXIF capture timestamps (normalize_exif_datetime) and media metadata into a local SQLite database.
  • YuNet Detection + SFace 128-D Face Recognition (face_engine.py:31, face_engine.py:29): Aligns 5-point facial landmarks via cv2.FaceDetectorYN (YuNet) and extracts $128$-dimensional feature embeddings $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^{128}$ via cv2.FaceRecognizerSF (SFace). Faces whose cosine similarity meets or exceeds MATCH_THRESHOLD = 0.360 are clustered as the same identity: $$\text{sim}(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 \, \|\mathbf{v}\|_2} \ge 0.360$$
  • YOLO11n Semantic Concept Tag Mapper (detector.py:23): Maps natural-language concept queries directly to COCO object detection classes via the CONCEPT_TO_YOLO_CLASSES dictionary.
  • Knowledge-Graph (FAMILY_GRAPH) Multimodal RAG Agent (gemini_agent.py:23, server.py:30): Injects structured metadata graphs into a multimodal agent to power natural-language media retrieval.
