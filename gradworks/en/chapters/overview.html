GoogleDrive 3 Pillars & 6 Operating Charter Rules

The top-level workspace charter (GoogleDrive/GEMINI.md) and the research monorepo charter (Development/GEMINI.md) enforce strict Separation of Concerns so that decades of research code, patents, papers, personal financial ledgers, and photo archives never contaminate one another or leak externally. Click any node in the architecture diagram below to inspect its detailed specification and verification rules.

Summary of the 6 Workspace Integrity Rules (GoogleDrive/GEMINI.md)
  • Rule 1 · Separation of Concerns: Media files reside strictly in Photos/, research and engineering code in Development/, and academic/administrative documents in Documents/.
  • Rule 2 · Zero-ZIP Policy: All archive files (.zip, .rar, .7z, etc.) are strictly prohibited inside Photos/ to prevent Google Photos backup omissions.
  • Rule 3 · Zero Non-Media Policy: Non-media documents (.pdf, .hwp, .pptx, .xlsx) are never placed inside Photos/.
  • Rule 4 · Purity of Daily Feed: Photos/Daily/ stores only direct camera-captured photos and videos, while screen captures (Screenshots/) and web-saved images (Downloads/) are quarantined separately.
  • Rule 5 · Privacy Gate: Documents/Personal/ and 1_Household_Finance/Data/*.xlsx are strictly excluded from Git tracking and external documentation under all circumstances.
  • Rule 6 · Cloud Shortcut & EXIF Integrity: Cloud shortcuts (.gsheet/.gdoc) and original photo EXIF DateTimeOriginal metadata are preserved without modification.
