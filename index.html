  • Dual-Path Streaming & Zero-Shutter-Lag Generative Capture: Architected a dual-path pipeline where the single resident latent stream concurrently drives fluid viewfinder preview (30.0 FPS) and on-demand Zero-Shutter-Lag (ZSL) generative capture; extracted structural edge priors directly on the host SoC to condition frozen downstream diffusion backbones (SD 2.1 U-Net + ControlNet) with zero raw-frame bus transmission (0 KB overhead), zero secondary sensor exposure, and zero encoder re-execution, ensuring complete on-device data privacy.
