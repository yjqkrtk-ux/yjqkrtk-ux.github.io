3. White-Box Computational Latent Signal Processing: Step-by-Step Radiometric & Colorimetric Operators and Iterative Manifold Refinement in 16-Channel VAEs
Authors: Yongjoon Song, Han-sol Lee, Jeisung Lee, Yeolmin Seong • Samsung Electronics, Republic of Korea

Establishes a unified 3-class taxonomy of latent image processing operators—(I) Pointwise Linear/Affine, (II) Pointwise Non-Linear (`1:4:1` Simpson Quadrature + $3 imes 3$ Secant Jacobians), and (III) Spatial Neighborhood Kernels (13D AC Spectral Multipliers + $3 imes 3$ Cross-Cell Bilateral/ODE Stencils)—directly on the H/8 × W/8 × 16 latent grid of genuine hardware sensor Bayer RAWs, boosting 10-camera mean sRGB PSNR from 25.04 dB (0-Net) to 27.18 dB (4-Step Refiner).
