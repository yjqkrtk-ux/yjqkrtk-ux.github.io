Sub-Micron Pixel Physics: Airy Diffraction, Full-Well Capacity & Shot-Noise SNR

As pixel pitch $p$ scales down from $0.60\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (HP2) to $0.56\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (HP3/HP9) and $0.50\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (HP5), raw image formation is governed by three coupled physical constraints: optical diffraction from the lens aperture, photodiode Full-Well Capacity ($N_{\text{FWC}}$), and Poisson photon shot noise:

$$d_{\text{Airy}} = 2.44\,\lambda\,N, \qquad \text{SNR}(E) = 20\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{\eta_{\text{QE}}\,p^2\,E\,t_{\text{int}}}{\sqrt{\eta_{\text{QE}}\,p^2\,E\,t_{\text{int}} + \sigma_{\text{dark}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{read}}^2}}\right), \qquad \text{DR}_{\max} = 20\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{N_{\text{FWC}}}{\sigma_{\text{read}}}\right)$$
  • Optical Diffraction Exceeds Single-Pixel Pitch: At green wavelength $\lambda = 0.55\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ and mobile lens aperture $N = f/1.7$, the diffraction-limited Airy disk diameter is $d_{\text{Airy}} \approx 2.28\text{ }\mu\text{m}$. Across a $4\times 4$ Hexadeca cluster, the macro-block width is $4p = 2.40\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (HP2), $2.24\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (HP3/HP9), and $2.00\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (HP5). Thus, a single point light source spreads optical energy across an entire $4\times 4$ same-color neighborhood!
  • Photodiode Area & Dual-VTG / DTI Silicon Engineering: Shrinking $p$ from $0.60\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ to $0.50\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ reduces single-pixel photon-collecting area $A_{\text{pix}} = p^2$ by $30.6\%$ ($0.36\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2 \to 0.25\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$). To prevent saturation and inter-pixel color bleeding, ISOCELL sensors pair Front/Back-Side Deep Trench Isolation (DTI) with Dual Vertical Transfer Gate (D-VTG) and high-refractive micro-lenses.
  • Tetra²pixel ($4\times 4$ Hexadeca CFA) Three-Mode Readout Hierarchy:
    1. Mode A — $4\times 4$ Hexadeca Binning (12.5 MP):Sums $16$ adjacent same-color photodiodes in charge/analog domain into a $2.0\text{–}2.4\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ virtual super-pixel ($16\times$ photon count, $+12\text{ dB}$ shot-noise SNR boost) with native Bayer ordering for extreme low-light Nightography and 8K/4K 60fps video.
    2. Mode B — $2\times 2$ Tetra Binning (50 MP & 2× In-Sensor Zoom): Groups $2\times 2$ sub-blocks ($1.0\text{–}1.2\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ effective pitch), yielding a $2\times 2$ Tetra mosaic reconstructed via 4-cell remosaicing for everyday 50MP stills and lossless 2× crop zoom.
    3. Mode C — $1\times 1$ Unbinned Readout (200 MP & 4× In-Sensor Zoom): Reads all $200\text{M}$ individual photodiodes ($0.50\text{–}0.60\text{ }\mu\text{m}$) as a $4\times 4$ Hexadeca mosaic—requiring DL Hexadeca Remosaic (HP3/HP9/HP5) or in-sensor QMSC (HP2) to reconstruct full 200MP Bayer/RGB or a central 12.5MP lossless 4× telephoto crop.

World-First DL Hexadeca (16-Cell) Remosaic (HP3/HP9) & In-Sensor QMSC (HP2)

Why do classical directional interpolation filters fail on a $4\times 4$ Hexadeca CFA? In a standard $1\times 1$ Bayer array, every pixel is at most $1\text{ px}$ away from missing color channels. In a $4\times 4$ Hexadeca mosaic, an interior pixel (such as $(1,1)$ or $(2,2)$ inside a $4\times 4$ Red block) is separated by up to $3\text{–}4$ sub-micron pixels from the nearest Green or Blue sample—exceeding the local Nyquist correlation radius and causing severe checkerboard phase artifacts, zippering, and false color moiré under linear/cubic interpolation.

In a $4\times 4$ Hexadeca mosaic $I_{\text{RAW}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times H \times W}$, each pixel coordinate $(y, x)$ has a periodic spatial phase $(\Delta y, \Delta x) = (y \bmod 4,\; x \bmod 4) \in \{0,1,2,3\}^2$ relative to its $4\times 4$ macro-block boundary. Applying a standard $3\times 3$ convolution directly on $I_{\text{RAW}}$ applies identical weights to corner pixels (adjacent to two neighboring colors), edge pixels (adjacent to one neighboring color), and center pixels (surrounded entirely by the same color).

To make the neural operator strictly phase-aware and maximize mobile NPU throughput, the input mosaic is decomposed via periodic PixelUnshuffle ($\mathcal{U}_{4\times 4}$) into a 16-channel phase-homogeneous tensor at $\frac{1}{16}$ spatial resolution:

$$\mathcal{U}_{4\times 4} : \mathbb{R}^{1 \times H \times W} \;\longrightarrow\; \mathbb{R}^{16 \times \frac{H}{4} \times \frac{W}{4}}, \qquad \mathbf{X}_{c}(u, v) = I_{\text{RAW}}\!\left(4u + \lfloor c/4 \rfloor,\; 4v + (c \bmod 4)\right), \quad c \in \{0,\dots,15\}$$

Inside the low-resolution latent space $\left(\frac{H}{4} \times \frac{W}{4}\right)$, $N$ residual NPU blocks $\mathcal{G}_{\theta}$ learn cross-phase directional attention and high-frequency luminance/chrominance transfer before periodic PixelShuffle ($\mathcal{U}_{4\times 4}^{-1}$) reassembles the full 200MP Bayer/RGB grid $\hat{I}_{\text{Bayer}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1\times H\times W}$:

$$\hat{I}_{\text{Bayer}} = \mathcal{U}_{4\times 4}^{-1}\!\left(\mathbf{W}_{\text{out}} * \mathcal{G}_{\theta}^{(N)}\!\left(\mathcal{U}_{4\times 4}(I_{\text{RAW}}),\; \mathbf{G}_{\text{WB}}\right)\right) + I_{\text{skip}}$$

Key Takeaways · Chapter 01 (200MP ISOCELL Sensor Development)

  1. Sub-Micron Scaling (0.60µm → 0.56µm → 0.50µm): Across ISOCELL HP2, HP3/HP9, and HP5 (world's smallest 200MP sensor), Tetra²pixel ($4\times 4 = 16$-cell) hardware binning maximizes low-light SNR while preserving 200MP spatial sampling in daylight and telephoto crop zoom.
  2. World-First DL Hexadeca Remosaic & In-Sensor QMSC: Periodic 16-channel PixelUnshuffle phase decomposition eliminates $4\times 4$ phase boundary artifacts, powering Galaxy S23 Ultra (HP2 QMSC 4× zoom) and vivo X200 Pro ZEISS 200MP Telephoto (HP9 DL 16-cell remosaic).
