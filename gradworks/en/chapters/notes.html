Single-Source-of-Truth Build Principle & 5-Stage Automated Verification

The entire guidebook (docs/index.html, docs/chapters/*.html, docs/css/*, docs/js/*) is generated deterministically by docs/build_spec_portal.py rather than manual HTML editing, and must pass all five integrity gates below before any deployment artifact is updated.

Gate 1 · Source Link & Line Anchor Verification

Every src() call in the text is checked to verify that the target file exists on disk and that the searched function, class, or constant resolves to its exact 1-based line anchor (#Lxx).

Gate 2 · KaTeX Math & HTML DOM Integrity

Checks brace balance across all display and inline LaTeX equations, blocks unescaped non-ASCII characters inside math mode, and validates matching open/close tags via Python's HTMLParser.

Gate 3 · Privacy Gate (Enforcing Rule 5)

Immediately aborts the build if even a single reference to private files under Documents/Personal/, local financial ledgers (Data/*.xlsx), or restricted personal identifiers appears in the output.
