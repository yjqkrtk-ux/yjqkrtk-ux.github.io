Why Are Zero-ZIP and Purity of the Daily Feed Critical? Cloud photo backup clients cannot inspect the contents of .zip, .7z, or .tar archives, meaning any media left inside compressed bundles is omitted from timeline indexing (Rule 2: Zero-ZIP Policy). Furthermore, mixing screen captures or downloaded web images into Daily/ pollutes chronological timelines and degrades AI face/scene clustering quality, so they are strictly segregated into Screenshots/ and Downloads/ (Rule 4: Purity of Daily Feed).

AI Photo Memory Studio (4_Photo_Memory_Studio) — Read-Only Indexing & Dual Viewer

Development/5_Household_and_Life_Automation/4_Photo_Memory_Studio/ (4_Photo_Memory_Studio/server.py) mounts the Photos/ storage in strict Read-Only mode to preserve original file and EXIF integrity (Rule 6), combining a local SQLite index database with a multimodal vision pipeline for searching and comparing large photo and video collections.

$$d_{\cos}(\mathbf{e}_i, \mathbf{e}_j) = 1 - \frac{\mathbf{e}_i \cdot \mathbf{e}_j}{\|\mathbf{e}_i\|_2 \, \|\mathbf{e}_j\|_2} \le \tau_{\text{face}} \quad (\mathbf{e} \in \mathbb{R}^{128}, \; \tau_{\text{face}} = 0.55)$$
where \(\mathbf{e}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{128}\) is the 128-dimensional L2-normalized embedding vector extracted by the SFace network from facial regions detected by OpenCV YuNet (confidence \(\ge 0.60\)). Hierarchical clustering under cosine distance \(d_{\cos} \le 0.55\) is fused with YOLO11n object tags and Graph RAG to answer natural-language queries.
