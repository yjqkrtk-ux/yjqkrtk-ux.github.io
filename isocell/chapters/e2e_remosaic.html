Overcoming the 200MP Serial Capture Bottleneck (Gen 2 Parallel Architecture)

A single 200MP 10-bit RAW frame (`16,320 × 12,288`) occupies ~250 MB in unpacked 16-bit memory, and a 3-channel 200MP RGB/YUV frame occupies 600 MB. In a conventional serial pipeline, the system must complete full-frame Remosaic before starting ISP tone/color processing—incurring multiple 200MP DRAM read/write round-trips and ~3 seconds of capture latency.

$$T_{\text{serial}} = T_{\text{RAW-DMA}} + T_{\text{DL-Remosaic}} + T_{\text{DRAM- flush}} + T_{\text{SW/HW-ISP}} \;\approx\; 3.0\text{ s}$$ $$T_{\text{E2E-Parallel}} = \max_{k \in \{\text{NPU},\,\text{GPU},\,\text{ISP}\}}\!\left(T_{\text{tile}, k}\right) \cdot N_{\text{tiles}} + T_{\text{pipe-fill}} \;\approx\; 1.0\text{ s}\quad (\text{Up to } 3\times \text{ faster})$$

As publicly highlighted by Samsung Semiconductor Global (“Samsung Electronics Introduces ISOCELL Zoom Anyplace & E2E AI Remosaic Solutions for the 200-Megapixel Image Sensor”), E2E AI Remosaic fuses DL Remosaic and ISP stages into a parallelized, tile-streamed heterogeneous pipeline across mobile CPU, GPU, NPU, and ISP engines (Qualcomm Snapdragon & MediaTek Dimensity).

Key Takeaways · Chapter 02

  1. End-to-End Co-Optimization: By overlapping NPU neural remosaicing with GPU/ISP color and tone processing at the tile level (Gen 2), 200MP capture latency drops from ~3.0s to ~1.0s while eliminating intermediate full-frame DRAM spills.
  2. Gen 4 Multi-Frame Neural Restoration: Jointly fusing multiple non-Bayer burst frames directly inside the DL Remosaic network recovers sub-Nyquist spatial detail and suppresses temporal noise on flagship AP NPUs (HONOR Magic 9 series).
